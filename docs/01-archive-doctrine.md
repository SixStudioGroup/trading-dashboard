# Archive Doctrine

Archivum is the controlled memory of an engagement.

Its purpose is to preserve artefacts, decisions, evidence, reports, exports, and closure records in a form that remains readable outside the generating application.

Archivum follows four principles: portability, isolation, traceability, and closure.

Portability means every important artefact can leave the originating tool as Markdown, JSON, PDF-ready output, or another agreed open format.

Isolation means one client or engagement receives one private repository and one access boundary.

Traceability means every controlled artefact records its source, version, classification, owner, and status.

Closure means every engagement ends with review, handover, retention instruction, and eventual archive or deletion.

Archivum does not replace enterprise systems. Where a client already uses Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, Google Drive, Notion, Azure DevOps, or another approved platform, Archivum acts as the portable handover and evidence layer unless the client directs otherwise.

The central Archivum repo contains only doctrine and reusable placeholders. Client data belongs only in client-specific private repositories.
