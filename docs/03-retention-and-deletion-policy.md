# Retention and Deletion Policy

Archivum uses contract-led retention.

The default retention period is twelve months after formal engagement closure unless the client agreement, law, insurance requirement, or written client instruction requires a different period.

Retention begins only after final handover and sign-off. During retention, the repository remains private, access is minimised, and routine editing stops.

A client may request earlier deletion. The request must be confirmed in writing, the repository owner must verify authority, and the deletion action must be recorded outside the client repo if evidence of compliance is required.

At the end of retention, the Founder / CTO decides one of three outcomes: extend retention with approval, archive under an agreed basis, or delete.

Deletion includes the GitHub repository and any operator-held working copies or exported packages covered by the request. Material that must be retained for legal, contractual, accounting, or insurance reasons is handled under the applicable obligation and disclosed to the client where required.

The central Archivum template repository never receives client content and is not part of client deletion activity.
