## What changed because of this

- Scan groups by rule state and does not rank. There is no score to mistake for merit.
- The planner requires your own thesis and numeric stop, and nothing is prefilled.
- New trades default to paper. Paper criteria: 30 closed trades, positive expectancy after costs, 90% plan-following. Meeting them is a process milestone, not proof of an edge.
- Crypto is labelled unproven and sits in the footer, not the main navigation.

Your own paper record is the evidence that counts. None of this is financial advice.
