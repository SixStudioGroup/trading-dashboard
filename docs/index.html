ZenCloud public operating note

Magister Automatorum

A founder FinOps model for building an AI-assisted product estate with limited cash, controlled subscriptions, many repositories, and disciplined operating memory.

The founder is the brain. AI tools are specialised shells. GitHub preserves the source. Obsidian preserves the memory. Cloudflare protects the edge.

Visit ZenCloud Advisory View repository

The constraint

A founder-led product estate has to operate under real cash limits. The current posture is less than AUD $1,000 available, monthly AI subscriptions, free or low-cost infrastructure, and a strong preference for manual control before expensive automation.

The method

Route work by weight. ChatGPT handles planning and light execution. Codex handles scoped implementation. Claude handles long-form writing and deep repo work. Experimental agents stay sandboxed until they prove value.

The outcome

The estate can move fast without losing the decision trail. Every session should produce a public asset, repo improvement, product increment, commercial lead path, operating note, or reusable governance artefact.

Why this matters

This is not tool sprawl. It is controlled orchestration. The founder keeps authority while assigning work to the lowest-cost tool capable of producing the required quality. The model protects scarce cash, reduces repeated context loading, and turns daily AI sessions into durable assets.

The product idea is Magister Automatorum: a low-cost founder operating system for AI-assisted product delivery, grounded in GitHub, governed through Obsidian, deployed through lightweight hosting, and measured through revenue channels.
