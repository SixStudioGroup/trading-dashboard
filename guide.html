Operator Manual

Use SixQuant to decide, not to chase.

SixQuant is a manual trading decision cockpit. It plans, records, and reviews. Execution stays external.

Core ruleIf the idea did not start in SixQuant, do not execute it externally.
Execution boundaryNo broker API. No order placement. No autopilot.
Operating loopScan → Analyse → Decide → Record → Review

Boundary

SixQuant is decision support only.

SixQuant does not provide financial advice, place orders, connect to brokers, manage custody, rebalance positions, or guarantee outcomes. It exists to slow the trader down enough to make disciplined, reviewable decisions.
